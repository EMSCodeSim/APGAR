Teaching notes

  • APGAR is assessed at 1 and 5 minutes (and later if needed); this sim is for recognition practice, not timing.
  • Appearance 1 shows blue hands/feet only; Appearance 0 adds central cyanosis.
  • Pulse drives the chest glow rate. Grimace response is clearest after Stimulate.
  • Activity changes limb posture; Respiration changes chest rise and optional cry.
